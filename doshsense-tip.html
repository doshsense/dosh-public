Why Most People Get Financial Advice Wrong

Most financial advice is given without knowing your situation, goals, timeline, or risk tolerance. Generic advice applied to a specific situation is often worse than no advice at all. The rule that works for one person can be damaging for another.

Before applying any piece of financial advice, ask: does this actually apply to my situation? The answer is often no - and that's fine. Your situation is your own. The best financial decisions are always contextual.
