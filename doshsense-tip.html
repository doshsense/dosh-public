The Hidden Cost of Financial Avoidance

Not opening the app, not checking the balance, not looking at the statement - financial avoidance feels protective because what you can't see can't stress you. But it makes every problem worse by removing the information needed to address it.

Avoidance doesn't make the debt smaller. It just means facing the same problem later with less time and more anxiety. Clarity - however uncomfortable - is always less expensive than the alternative. Open the thing. Look at the number. It is almost never as bad as the anxiety suggests.
