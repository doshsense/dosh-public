How to Get More From the Money You Already Have

There's a third lever beyond earning more or spending less - getting more value from money already flowing through your life. Switching a savings account from 0.5% to 4.5% costs nothing. Consolidating debt reduces payments. Reviewing insurance saves hundreds without reducing cover.

These optimisations don't require extra income or lifestyle cuts. They require one thing: the habit of reviewing rather than defaulting. Most people stay with the same bank and rate long after better options appeared - not out of loyalty, but inertia.
