The Spending Leak You Don't Notice

Most budget problems aren't caused by one large decision. They're caused by dozens of small ones that individually feel irrelevant. The extra coffee. The upgraded meal deal. The in-app purchase. None feel like spending decisions - they feel like the cost of getting through the day.

Spending leaks are the financial equivalent of a dripping tap. Each drop is invisible. Together they drain something significant. Seeing the monthly total changes the decision. The leak doesn't stop itself.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Open your Everyday Flex in DoshSense and look for the highest-frequency small transactions this month. Coffee, snacks, small top-ups. Add them up. That total is your spending leak. You don't have to eliminate it - just decide if it's worth what it's costing.
