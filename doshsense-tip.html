What to Do With a Windfall

Unexpected money is spent faster and with less deliberation than earned income. A bonus, refund, or gift rarely gets treated with the same care as a paycheck. The most effective approach: do nothing with it for 30 days. Park it and let the excitement settle.

Then apply a simple split - some toward your highest-priority financial goal, some toward something you'd genuinely enjoy, the rest as a buffer. Give some of it a job that serves your future. Enjoy some of it without guilt. A windfall handled well can change a financial trajectory.
