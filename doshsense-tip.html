The Hidden Cost of Financial Indecision

Not making a financial decision is itself a decision - and it usually has a cost. Money sitting in the wrong account. Cover that no longer fits your life. A debt strategy never chosen, paying more interest than necessary.

A reasonable decision made today and adjusted later is worth far more than a perfect decision made never. The cost of waiting for perfect information is almost always higher than the cost of a small course correction. Motion beats paralysis every time.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Open DoshSense and name one financial decision you've been putting off. It could be switching a provider, opening a savings account, or choosing a debt repayment order. Write it down. Then make the decision today - not perfectly, just well enough. You can course-correct later. You can't get back the time spent not deciding.
