Today's Money Insight
The Role of Community in Financial Progress

Research on behaviour change consistently shows that social accountability accelerates progress. Telling someone your financial goal makes you more likely to reach it. Having a partner who shares your financial values removes one of the biggest sources of conflict and drift.

Find at least one person with whom you can talk about money honestly. Not to compare or compete - to share goals, celebrate progress, and hold each other accountable. The financial journey is harder alone than it needs to be.
