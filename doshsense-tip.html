The True Value of Financial Patience

Patience is one of the most powerful forces in personal finance and one of the least celebrated. Compound interest rewards it. Markets reward it. Habits reward it. The person who invests consistently for thirty years without trying to time the market will almost always outperform the person who invests more but inconsistently.

Financial patience is not passivity - it's the discipline of staying on a good plan when short-term noise makes it feel wrong. Urgency has its place. But patience is what turns good decisions into great outcomes.
