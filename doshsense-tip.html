The Financial Case for Simplicity

Complexity in personal finance is rarely an asset. More accounts, more products, more strategies - each adds management overhead and increases the chance of something slipping through. The most financially secure people tend to have simpler setups than you'd expect.

Simple systems get used. Complex ones get avoided. A simple system used consistently beats a perfect system used occasionally every time. The goal isn't impressive architecture - it's removing every barrier between intention and action.
