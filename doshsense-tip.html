The Psychology of a Pay Rise - Why More Never Feels Like Enough

Within months of a pay rise, spending adjusts upward, expectations reset, and the feeling of having more evaporates. The hedonic treadmill keeps moving and enough stays just out of reach.

The window before spending adjusts is the moment of maximum opportunity. Redirecting even half of an increase to savings before lifestyle inflation absorbs it locks in real progress. Most people spend the whole rise. The few who save part of it first change their trajectory.
