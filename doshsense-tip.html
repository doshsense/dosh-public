Today's Money Insight
Why Every Dollar Needs a Job

Unallocated money disappears - not through any single large purchase, but through the accumulation of small unmemorable ones. Give every dollar a job before it arrives: savings automated, bills covered, discretionary budget set. What's left is genuinely yours to spend without guilt.

Zero-based thinking - income minus all allocations equals zero - isn't about spending nothing. It's about making sure nothing disappears. Every dollar accounted for means no money comes from nowhere.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Open DoshSense this month and add up your three category totals plus your savings. Does the sum match your income? If there's a gap - that's the money with no job. It's not a problem to feel bad about; it's a problem to solve. Give that gap a job today: savings, debt, or a named discretionary fund.
