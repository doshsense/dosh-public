The Value of Knowing Your Why

Every financial goal is more powerful when attached to a reason that matters. Saving money is abstract. Saving so your children don't inherit the financial stress you grew up with is personal and motivating. Your why is the fuel that keeps the habit running when motivation dips - and it always dips eventually.

Write down your financial why today. Not the goal - the reason behind it. The person you're doing it for. The life you're trying to build. That reason is more powerful than any technique in this course.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Open DoshSense and look at your goals. For each one - write next to it the why behind it. Not the number. The reason. Keep that somewhere visible. Next time you're tempted to skip the check-in or let the savings slide, read the why first. It changes the decision.
