Understanding the True Cost of Lifestyle Creep

Lifestyle creep ensures that no matter how much income grows, the gap between income and savings stays the same - or narrows. Each upgrade feels deserved. Together they absorb every raise before it reaches savings.

The antidote isn't to never upgrade - it's to upgrade deliberately. When income grows, decide consciously how much goes to lifestyle and how much goes to the future. Even a 50-50 split captures the raise without surrendering it entirely to creep.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Open DoshSense and compare your Lifestyle Flex now to six months ago. Has it grown? If yes - is that growth deliberate or was it just absorbed automatically? There's no wrong answer, only an honest one. Deliberate lifestyle upgrades are fine. Unconscious creep is what today's lesson is about.
