The Financial Upside of a Crisis You Managed Well

Every financial crisis that doesn't derail you makes you more resilient to the next one. An emergency fund that got used is a fund that did its job. A month that ran over but was caught early and corrected is a monitoring system working.

Most people treat setbacks as evidence their system is failing. The more accurate reading is that they're stress tests revealing what's working. The goal isn't to avoid all difficulty - it's to build habits that make difficulty manageable. Every hard month you navigate rather than avoid is evidence of that.
