How to Handle Financial Envy Without It Costing You

Financial envy is normal. The problem isn't the feeling - it's what it can trigger: spending to signal status, debt to keep up, or abandoning a sound plan for a shinier one. The most useful response is curiosity rather than comparison.

When you notice envy, ask: do I actually want that thing, or do I want what I imagine it represents? Most of the time the feeling you want is more reliably reached by building genuine financial security than by acquiring whatever triggered the envy. Other people's choices are made with context you don't have. Focus on your plan.
