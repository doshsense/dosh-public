The Difference Between a Budget and a Spending Plan

A budget is a restriction: how much you're allowed to spend. A spending plan is an intention: how you've decided to use your money. One feels like a cage. The other feels like a map. Both allocate money across categories - the difference is psychological but significant.

When you've already decided how much goes to Lifestyle Flex, spending within it doesn't feel like sacrifice. It feels like freedom within a framework you designed yourself.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Open DoshSense and look at your three categories. Try reframing them not as limits but as decisions already made. Recurring Bills: committed. Everyday Flex: planned. Lifestyle Flex: chosen. You designed this framework. Spending within it isn't a restriction - it's execution. That reframe changes how every transaction feels.
