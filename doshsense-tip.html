The Art of Spending Without Regret

Regret-free spending has three ingredients. It's planned - you knew it was coming and accounted for it. It's chosen - you selected it over alternatives. It's proportionate - the amount matches the value received.

When all three are present, you can spend freely without guilt. The goal isn't restriction. It's the confidence that comes from knowing your spending reflects your actual values rather than your impulsive ones.
