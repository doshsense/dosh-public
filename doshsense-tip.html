The True Cost of a High-Interest Credit Card

A credit card at 22% interest is a loan on every purchase you don't clear in full. A $2,000 balance paid at the minimum rate can take over five years to clear and cost more in interest than the original balance was worth.

The card company wins every month you carry a balance. The only winning move is to clear in full each month - or treat the balance as the highest-priority debt in the budget. Every dollar over the minimum is a dollar of future interest you'll never have to pay.
