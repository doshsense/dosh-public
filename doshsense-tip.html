The Real Reason Most Budgets Fail

Most budgets fail not because the numbers are wrong but because they're built on an idealised version of life. A budget that assumes you'll never eat out or have an unexpected expense isn't a budget - it's a wish list. When reality diverges, the whole thing gets abandoned.

A realistic budget you stick to imperfectly is worth infinitely more than a perfect budget you abandon after three weeks. Design for reality, not aspiration. The goal is something you can live with.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Look at last month's Everyday and Lifestyle Flex in DoshSense. Were the totals close to what you expected - or significantly over? If over, that gap is your budget telling you it needs adjusting. Don't fight reality. Adjust the budget to fit your actual life, then work from there.
