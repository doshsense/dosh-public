Today's Money Insight
The Hidden Cost of Keeping Up Appearances

A significant proportion of consumer spending is driven by the need to appear a certain way. The car that signals success. The holiday that looks good on social media. These aren't inherently wrong - but spending to manage others' perceptions is one of the least satisfying ways to use money.

"Would I buy this if nobody would ever know?" is a useful test. If the answer is no, you're buying the perception, not the thing. That's worth knowing before you commit.
