The Power of the Monthly Financial Check-In

A monthly financial check-in is one of the highest-return habits in personal finance. Not a full audit - just thirty structured minutes at the same time each month. Income received. Bills paid. Spending by category. Progress toward goals. One thing to improve.

Monthly reviews over a year tell you your direction, your velocity, and your patterns. They catch problems early - a category creeping, a goal falling behind, a bill that changed without notice. Most people do this once a year at tax time when it's too late to course correct.
