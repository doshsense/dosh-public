Understanding Wants You Can Plan For

Planned spending is fundamentally different from impulse spending even when the category looks the same. A holiday saved for over six months is Lifestyle Flex done right. The difference is the gap between wanting something and buying it - closed slowly and deliberately.

Building a deliberate "wants" allocation into your budget removes the shame cycle of impulse spending and replaces it with anticipation. Wanting things is normal. Planning for them is the skill.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Is there something you want in the next three to six months? Name it, estimate the cost, and open DoshSense to see how much Lifestyle Flex you'd need to redirect each month to fund it. That's planned spending in action - and it feels completely different from buying on impulse.
