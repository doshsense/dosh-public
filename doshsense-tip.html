The Art of the Annual Financial Review

Once a year, review your entire financial picture in one sitting. Income versus last year. Savings rate. Debt balances. Insurance. Net worth change. Goals achieved and missed. The annual review reveals trends only visible over a longer timeframe - things the monthly check-in can't catch.

Set a date now. Year end works well. So does your birthday. The specific date matters less than having one - and keeping it.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Open your calendar right now and block one hour for your annual financial review. Pick a date - year end, your birthday, any anchor that recurs. Then open DoshSense and note down five numbers to compare against at that review: Recurring Bills total, monthly savings, biggest spending category, net worth estimate, and one active goal. That list is your starting point.
