Why the Second Year of Any Financial Habit Is Easier Than the First

The first year of a financial habit is the hardest - building the system, overcoming resistance, learning your own patterns. The second year shifts the question from "can I do this?" to "how do I do this better?" The friction is gone. The system runs.

You are approaching the end of your first year. Everything you've built is now the foundation for a second year that will be easier, more productive, and more rewarding. The hard part is behind you.
