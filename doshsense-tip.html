Why Minimum Payments Are a Trap Designed to Last

Minimum payments exist to keep you paying, not to help you get out of debt. At minimum payment rates, a $3,000 balance at 22% interest can take over a decade to clear and cost more in interest than the original balance.

The moment you pay even $20 more than the minimum, you start winning. That extra amount reduces the principal faster, which reduces the interest, which makes every future payment more effective. Small overpayments compound powerfully in your favour.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Open your Recurring Bills in DoshSense. Do you have a credit card payment in there? If it's set to the minimum - look at your Lifestyle Flex this month and find $20 or $30 to redirect toward it instead. That one change, made permanent, significantly shortens your timeline to being free of it.
