The Difference Between a Financial Goal and a Financial Wish

"I'd like to save more" is a wish. "I will save $300 a month by automating a transfer on the 1st, giving me $3,600 by year end" is a goal. One is a vague intention. The other is a plan.

Goals have four things wishes don't: a specific number, a defined timeline, a clear mechanism, and a consequence for missing it that you care about. Without all four, even the best intentions dissolve under daily life.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Open DoshSense and identify one financial wish you've been carrying. Now convert it into a goal - give it a number, a date, and a mechanism. Write it down somewhere you'll see it. That conversion, done today, is one of the highest-value things you can do on day 101.
