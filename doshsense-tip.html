The Difference Between Being Broke and Feeling Broke

Being broke and feeling broke are not the same thing. Someone with $50,000 in savings but no visibility can feel anxious every day. Someone with modest income but a clear picture can feel genuinely in control. The feeling isn't primarily determined by the amount - it's determined by the clarity.

Financial calm is available at almost any income level to people who know exactly where they stand. Clarity precedes calm. Calm precedes good decisions.
