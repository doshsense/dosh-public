The Difference Between Price and Value

Price is what you pay. Value is what you get. A $10 meal shared with someone you love can have more value than a $60 meal eaten alone. A $200 pair of shoes that lasts five years has more value than four $60 pairs that don't.

The question before any significant purchase isn't "is this affordable?" It's "is this worth what I'm giving up to have it?" Those are very different questions with very different answers.
