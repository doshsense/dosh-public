What Financial Independence Actually Means

Financial independence doesn't have to mean never working again. The more useful definition is simpler: having enough margin that your choices are no longer entirely dictated by your next paycheck.

An emergency fund that absorbs shocks. No high-interest debt. Monthly outgoings that leave room to breathe. Decisions made by preference rather than necessity. None of that requires wealth - it requires consistency, a little margin, and the absence of emergencies compounding on each other.
