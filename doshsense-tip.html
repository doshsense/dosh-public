The Real Reason People Stay in Financial Situations They Hate

What keeps people stuck isn't a lack of information. It's that the change feels too large to start, short-term discomfort outweighs long-term benefit in the moment, or there's an identity attached to the current situation that makes changing it feel like losing something.

The antidote: make the first step so small it can't be refused. Look at one number. Cancel one subscription. Increase one payment by $10. Once the identity starts to shift - from someone who avoids money to someone who manages it - the bigger changes become possible. You're 119 days into that shift.
