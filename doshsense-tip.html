The Art of the Financial Reset

After a difficult month or a period of drift - do a reset. Not a punishment or dramatic overhaul. Just a deliberate pause to look at where things stand, acknowledge what happened, and recommit to the basics.

It takes twenty minutes. Look at last month's spending. Identify what drifted. Adjust the plan. Check the savings are going out. No guilt required. Financial resets are the maintenance that keeps a good system running. Reset regularly. Move on quickly.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Do a mini reset in DoshSense right now. Look at last month across all three categories. Pick the one that drifted most. Decide on one specific change for this month. That's it - reset complete. Five minutes, no drama, back on track.
