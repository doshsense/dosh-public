The Financial Benefits of Sleeping on It

Never make a significant financial decision at the end of a long day. Cognitive function and impulse control both deteriorate with fatigue. Decisions made when tired or emotionally overwhelmed are consistently worse than those made after rest.

A good financial decision made tomorrow morning is almost always better than a fast one made tonight. The deal will usually still be there. And your judgement will be sharper. Build the habit of sleeping on anything significant.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Opening DoshSense in the morning - as part of today's lesson from the start - is itself applying this principle. Your morning check-in happens when your judgement is sharpest. That timing is not accidental. It's one of the reasons daily morning engagement with your finances works better than evening reviews.
