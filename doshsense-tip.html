How to Make a Big Financial Decision Without Regret

Before any significant financial decision, ask four questions. One: what is the worst realistic outcome and can I live with it? Two: am I deciding from genuine desire or fear of missing out? Three: is this reversible? Four: would I make this decision if no one else would ever know?

If the answers hold up under a night's sleep and a conversation with someone whose financial judgement you respect, the decision is probably sound. If they don't, that's the information you needed before committing.
