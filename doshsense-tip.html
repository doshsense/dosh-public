Today's Money Insight
Why Your Spending Reflects Your Values - Whether You Meant It To or Not

Where your money goes is one of the most honest statements about what you actually value - as opposed to what you say you value. Without intentional spending structures, money flows toward the path of least resistance: easiest, most marketed, most immediately satisfying.

Look at last month's spending and ask: does this reflect the life I say I want? Not perfectly - that's not the goal. But directionally, does it point toward what matters? One deliberate redirect this month is enough to start closing the gap.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Open DoshSense and look at last month's Lifestyle Flex. Pick the three biggest transactions. For each one ask: does this reflect something I genuinely value, or did it just happen? No guilt either way - just honesty. Then identify one thing you'd redirect this month toward something that matters more. That's today's one action.
