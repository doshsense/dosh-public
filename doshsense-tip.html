Why Spending on Experiences Often Beats Spending on Things

Research consistently shows experiences deliver more lasting satisfaction than possessions. The holiday is remembered long after a purchase fades. Shared experiences build connection in ways things rarely do. And experiences are harder to compare - less vulnerable to the comparison trap.

When allocating Lifestyle Flex, asking "will I remember this in five years?" is a surprisingly useful filter. Most things won't pass it. The ones that do usually deserve the spend.
