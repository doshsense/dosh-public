How to Make a Financial Decision You Won't Regret

Most financial regret comes from decisions made too quickly - driven by emotion - or avoided too long until circumstances forced a worse outcome. Both are failures of process, not intelligence.

For any significant decision: write down what you're deciding, why now, what you'd gain, what you'd give up, and how you'd feel about it in twelve months. Ten minutes. That catches most of the common errors - impulse, avoidance, anchoring. You don't need certainty. You need enough time and an honest answer.
