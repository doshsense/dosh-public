Why Delayed Gratification Is a Learnable Skill

Delayed gratification is not a fixed personality trait - it's a muscle that strengthens with practice. Every time you apply the 24 hour rule, automate savings before spending, or choose the long-term option, it gets easier the next time.

95 days of checking in daily before spending is 95 repetitions of that same muscle. You are already stronger than you were on day one - whether or not it feels like it.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Next time you feel the urge to buy something unplanned - open DoshSense first. Look at this month's numbers. Then wait. That two-minute gap between the urge and the action is delayed gratification in practice. Do it enough times and it becomes automatic. You're already training it.
