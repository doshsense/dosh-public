The Cost of Not Knowing Your Insurance

Most people have insurance but couldn't tell you the limits, exclusions, or renewal dates of any of it. The cost of under-insurance only becomes visible when a claim falls short. The cost of over-insurance is paid monthly for years. Both are avoidable with one annual review.

For each policy you hold, know what it covers, what it doesn't, what it costs, and when it renews. Insurance you understand is a financial tool. Insurance you don't is a bill you hope never gets tested.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Open your Recurring Bills in DoshSense and find every insurance payment. List them out: health, home, car, life, income protection. For each one - do you know what it covers and when it renews? If there's a gap in your knowledge, that's the one to look at this week. Not all of them - just the one you know least about.
