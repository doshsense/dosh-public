How to Make Your Money Work While You Sleep

Passive financial progress starts smaller than most people expect - a savings account earning interest, debt reducing through a standing payment, an investment going out on payday before spending begins. None of these require active management once they're set up.

Each automated flow you establish is a permanent improvement that requires no further effort. Set one up today and it works for you tonight, and every night after that.
