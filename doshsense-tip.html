How to Think About Money When Times Are Tight

Financial pressure narrows focus to the immediate problem and crowds out longer-term thinking. The result is decisions that solve today's problem at the cost of next month's. These aren't failures of character - they're predictable responses to stress.

When money is tight: maintain visibility rather than lose it. Prioritise ruthlessly - Recurring Bills first, then everyday essentials, then everything else. Find the one cut that buys the most breathing room. Tight times are temporary. The habits made during them can last much longer.
