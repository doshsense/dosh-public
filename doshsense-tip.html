The Cost of Financial Avoidance

Not opening statements. Not checking balances. Not looking. Financial avoidance is one of the most expensive habits there is. Problems don't disappear when ignored - they grow. Interest compounds. Deadlines pass. Options narrow.

Avoidance is almost always driven by anxiety, not laziness. But the anxiety of not knowing is almost always worse than what's actually there. The moment you look, the anxiety has somewhere specific to go. Vague dread has no solution. Specific problems do. Looking is the hardest part.
