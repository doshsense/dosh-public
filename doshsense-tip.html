Why the Best Financial Plan Is the One You Actually Follow

A complex, optimised plan abandoned after six weeks produces worse outcomes than a simple, imperfect plan maintained for three years. Financial planning is not an intellectual exercise - it's a behavioural one.

Design your plan for the version of yourself who is tired and stressed - not the version sitting down on a Sunday with good intentions. The best plan has the fewest points of failure, the most automation, and the most forgiveness built in for bad months.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Opening DoshSense every morning is itself a plan designed for the tired version of you - one tap, three numbers, thirty seconds. That's why it works. Look at your numbers today and ask: is the rest of my financial system this simple? If not, what could you simplify to make it more likely to survive a difficult month?
