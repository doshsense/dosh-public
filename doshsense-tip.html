Building an Emergency Fund - Where to Start When You Have Nothing Spare

The gap between zero and three months' expenses feels so large that many people never start. Ignore the final target entirely at first. Focus on the first milestone: $500. That covers most genuine emergencies and removes the need to reach for a credit card.

Once you have $500, the next target is $1,000. Then one month. The psychological shift from zero to something is the most important one. Start smaller than the advice suggests. Start now.
