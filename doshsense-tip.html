What Happens to Your Finances When You Stop Paying Attention

Financial drift is slow, silent, and almost universal. A skipped review, a forgotten budget, a subscription that auto-renews, spending that expands to fill available space. None dramatic on their own. Together they can undo months of progress in a short time.

The daily check-in isn't just about making good decisions - it's about maintaining the visibility that makes good decisions possible. Never stop looking. The moment you do, the drift begins.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
Open DoshSense today and look specifically for drift - any category that's crept up without a conscious decision behind it. Compare this month's Lifestyle Flex to two months ago. Is it higher? If yes, is that deliberate or did it just happen? That question - deliberate or drift? - is the most useful one to ask regularly.
