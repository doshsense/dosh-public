The One Question That Changes Every Financial Decision

One question cuts through almost every financial decision more cleanly than any other: does this take me closer to or further from the life I want?

It connects every individual choice to the larger picture. It doesn't require a complex budget or financial plan - just knowing, at a broad level, what life you're actually trying to build. That clarity makes good financial decisions feel natural rather than forced.

Open DoshSense and look at this month's spending across all three categories. Apply the one question to the overall picture - does this month's spending take you closer to or further from the life you want? That answer, honest and clear, is the most useful financial reflection you can do today.
