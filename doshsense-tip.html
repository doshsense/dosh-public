The Financial Advantage of Starting Boring

The most effective personal finance systems are almost always boring. A savings account. A debt plan on autopilot. Three spending categories. Boring works because it removes the variables that cause failure - no account to obsess over, no strategy to second-guess, no decision fatigue.

Build something boring that runs itself. Then do the interesting parts of your life with the energy you save from not fiddling with it.

💡 Try It in DoshSense
DoshSense is deliberately boring - three categories, one daily check-in, one clear picture. Open it today and appreciate that simplicity. Then ask: is there anything in your wider financial life that's more complicated than it needs to be? One thing you could simplify to make it more likely to run without your attention?
